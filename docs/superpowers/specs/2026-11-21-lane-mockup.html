Band B taxable is 6,450. Extract 18/118 from that figure and you get 983.90; sum the four line taxes and you get 983.89. RRA wants the sum of lines, and the one-franc difference is what a load-bearing test in basket.rs exists to protect.

02 — When the VSDC drops

The lane stops selling. That is the feature.

C1 is not an error state we degrade through — offline selling is prohibited outright, so the lane must refuse. This is the screen that costs us a sale and keeps the certification.

Selling suspended

No connection to the VSDC

This till cannot issue a receipt until the connection is restored. The basket is held — nothing is lost.

What this mockup is claiming, and what it isn't

The layout above is C5's mandated shape: the SDC INFORMATION block with date, time, SDC ID, receipt number, internal data and receipt signature — the last two dashed every four characters — plus the CIS receipt number and the MRC.

  • Phase 2b builds the pipe: ESC/POS bytes, codepage, the QR command, paper cut and the drawer kick, behind a trait with a simulated implementation so CI needs no printer.
  • Phase 3 fills the content: the signature, internal data and QR payload all come from the VSDC's response. We cannot invent them — the values shown are placeholders.
  • Not yet decided: whether the QR is drawn with the printer's native command or rasterised by us, which is a per-model question and therefore waits for a real shop.
COPY, TRAINING and PRO FORMA must look different

C5 requires those three receipt types to be visually distinguishable from NORMAL. Our RcptTyCd already carries them; the printer layer owns making them look it.
